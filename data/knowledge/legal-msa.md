# Master Services Agreement: Key Terms

## Liability
Our standard MSA caps each party's liability at 12 months of fees.
Northwind does not accept unlimited liability.

## Governing law
New York law governs the MSA.

## Changes to the MSA
Any change to the standard MSA requires approval from Legal.
