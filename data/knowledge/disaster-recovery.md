# Backup & Disaster Recovery

## Backups
Daily encrypted backups, retained for 35 days, stored in a second region within
the same geography.

## Recovery objectives
Recovery point objective (RPO): 1 hour. Recovery time objective (RTO): 4 hours.
These apply to all plans.

## Testing
Disaster recovery is tested annually. The last test was in April 2026.
