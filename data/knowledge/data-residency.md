# Data Residency

## Regions
United States (AWS us-east-1 and us-west-2): all plans.
European Union (AWS eu-central-1, Frankfurt): Enterprise plan only, available since May 2026.
For EU tenants, customer data and backups stay in the EU.

## Other regions
No other regions are currently available. Asia-Pacific hosting is not offered.
