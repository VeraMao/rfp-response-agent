# Privacy & Compliance

## GDPR
Northwind complies with GDPR. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with EU Standard
Contractual Clauses is available to all customers.

## CCPA
Northwind complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act.

## PCI DSS
Northwind is not PCI DSS certified and does not process or store cardholder data.
Customers must not upload payment card data.
