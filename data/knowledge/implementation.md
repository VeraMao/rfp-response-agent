# Northwind Implementation Guide

## Standard timeline
A standard implementation takes about 90 days: 30 days setup and integration,
30 days data migration, 30 days training and go-live.

## Fast-track
Enterprise customers can choose Fast-track implementation, which completes in
45-60 days. Fast-track requires the customer to assign a full-time project
lead and provide data exports within the first two weeks.
