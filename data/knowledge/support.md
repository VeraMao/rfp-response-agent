# Customer Support

## Support hours
Standard plan: email support Monday-Friday, 9am-6pm ET; first response within
one business day.
Enterprise plan: 24/7 phone and email support; first response to P1 (critical)
issues within 1 hour.

## Languages
Support is provided in English. Spanish is available during business hours.
