# Northwind Product Capabilities

## Authentication
Northwind supports SAML 2.0 single sign-on. Tested identity providers: Okta,
Azure AD (Entra ID), and Google Workspace. SCIM user provisioning is available
on the Enterprise plan.

## Availability (SLA)
Standard plan: 99.5% monthly uptime SLA.
Enterprise plan: 99.9% monthly uptime SLA, with service credits if missed.

## Plans
Standard and Enterprise. Enterprise adds SCIM, the 99.9% SLA, and a dedicated
customer success manager.
