# Identity & Access

## Multi-factor authentication
MFA is available on all plans. Workspace admins can enforce MFA for every user.

## Audit logs
User and admin activity is logged. Audit logs are retained for 1 year.
Export through the API is available on the Enterprise plan.

## Sessions
Session timeout is configurable from 15 minutes to 24 hours.
