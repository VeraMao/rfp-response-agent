REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL - Retail Analytics
Issued by: Lumen Retail, IT Procurement
Response due: January 10, 2027

1. Background
Lumen Retail runs 220 stores in Singapore, Malaysia and Australia and wants one analytics platform
for store and e-commerce data.

2. How to respond
Answer each item in section 4.

3. Timeline
Contract signature expected in February 2027.

4. Requirements
4.1 Native integration with Snowflake.
4.2 Two-way integration with Salesforce.
4.3 The web application must meet WCAG 2.1 AA.
4.4 Mobile apps must work offline in stores with poor connectivity.
4.5 Customer data must be hosted in the Asia-Pacific region (Singapore preferred).
4.6 The vendor must guarantee at least 99.95% monthly uptime.
4.7 The platform must be live within 30 days of contract signature.
4.8 Our data must never be used to train AI models.
4.9 We must be able to switch off AI features.
4.10 Pricing must include unlimited users for a flat fee.
4.11 Provide a reference customer in the retail industry.
4.12 We require monthly billing.
