REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL - Analytics Platform
Issued by: Meridian Health, Procurement Office
Response due: December 1, 2026

1. Background
Meridian Health operates 14 clinics and wants a cloud analytics platform for
operational reporting. Vendors should answer each requirement in section 3.

2. Instructions
Please state for each requirement whether you meet it, and provide supporting detail.

3. Requirements
3.1 The platform must support single sign-on (SSO) with Okta.
3.2 The vendor must guarantee at least 99.9% monthly uptime.
3.3 The vendor must hold a current SOC 2 Type II report.
3.4 Implementation must be completed within 60 days of contract signature.
3.5 The vendor must be HIPAA compliant and willing to sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA).
